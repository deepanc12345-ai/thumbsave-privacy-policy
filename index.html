ThumbSave Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ThumbSave handles information when you use the ThumbSave Android application.

1. Information We Collect

ThumbSave does not require you to create an account and does not directly collect your name, email address, phone number, or other personal information.

When you enter a video URL, the URL is used only to retrieve the corresponding thumbnail image.

2. Advertising

ThumbSave uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and use information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, and usage information in accordance with Google's policies.

Advertising may be personalized or non-personalized depending on applicable settings, consent, and Google's advertising policies.

3. Downloaded Images

Thumbnail images downloaded through ThumbSave are saved to the user's device. ThumbSave does not upload downloaded images to our own servers.

4. Internet Access

The application requires internet access to retrieve thumbnail images and display advertisements.

5. Third-Party Services

ThumbSave uses third-party services such as Google AdMob. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

6. Children's Privacy

ThumbSave is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Google Play Store listing.
